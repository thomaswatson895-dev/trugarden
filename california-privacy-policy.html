California Consumer Privacy Notice

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Notice for California Residents supplements TruGreen’s general Privacy Policy and applies solely to all visitors, users, and customers residing in the State of California under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

1. Your Rights Under California Law

California residents have specific privacy rights regarding personal data:

  • Right to Know / Access: You have the right to request what personal information TruGreen has collected, used, and disclosed about you over the past 12 months.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request deletion of your personal data collected by TruGreen.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing: You have the right to direct us not to sell or share your personal information for targeted cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA privacy rights.

2. Submitting CCPA Privacy Requests

To exercise your California privacy rights, please submit your request online at Your Privacy Choices or call us toll-free at 1-844-567-9909.
